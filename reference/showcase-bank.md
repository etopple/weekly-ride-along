# Showcase Bank — segment 2 story material

Pre-anonymized, customer-safe descriptions of real things **you** have built,
fixed, or shipped. Every entry must be something that actually exists — no
fabrication. Mark `used: issue NNN` when an entry runs so entries rotate.

Add one entry every time something ships. Then the weeks where nothing
interesting happened still have a segment 2.

**Framing rules:**
- Ride-along voice: *"this week I watched an AI…"*, *"we finally killed the…"*
- One line of *"what this makes possible for a business like yours."*
- **No pitch.** The only CTA permitted is *"hit reply if this sparks an idea."*
- No product names, no vendor names, no customer names. Vertical + headcount
  only when a customer is involved — and drop the vertical too if the work
  came from an active deal.
- Prefer the unglamorous one. "It runs on its own now and nobody thinks about
  it" beats anything with the word *transformative* in it.

**Entry format:**

```
## N. <short title>
- **Real thing:** one paragraph, plain language.
- **What's possible line:** one sentence generalizing it to the reader's
  business.
- used: <issue number, or blank>
```

## 1. The monthly report that assembles itself
- **Real thing:** a report our team rebuilt by hand every month — same four
  sources, same six paragraphs, different numbers — is now assembled by an AI
  and read by a human before it goes anywhere. Most of a Friday became about
  twenty minutes of reading and correcting.
- **What's possible line:** any document your team rebuilds from the same
  sources every month is a candidate; the typing is not the work, the
  assembling is.

## 2. Walk around, talk, get a quote
- **Real thing:** our people walk a site taking phone photos and talking into
  a voice recorder. AI reads the photos, transcribes the audio, and produces
  a reconciled inventory — which becomes the quote. Nobody writes anything
  down twice.
- **What's possible line:** any "walk around and write it up later" job —
  inspections, estimates, audits — can become photos plus talking, with the
  writing-up done for you.

## 3. Ask your own systems a question in English
- **Real thing:** we gave our team a chat box that answers questions from our
  live business systems in plain language — with permissions enforced
  *underneath* the AI, so it physically cannot see data the person asking
  isn't allowed to see, no matter how the question is phrased.
- **What's possible line:** "ask your systems a question in English" is
  buildable today; the hard part is making sure the AI can't be talked past
  your permissions, and that part is solvable.

**Adding entries:** one paragraph, one possibility line, no names. If you
can't describe it without naming a customer or a product, it isn't ready for
the bank yet. If an entry came from an active deal, mark it and re-check the
